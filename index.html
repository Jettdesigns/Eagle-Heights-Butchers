The Mountain

Old Eagle Heights, at the top of the range.

Tamborine Mountain sits up in the rainforest-clad hinterland behind the Gold Coast, and Eagle Heights is its old heart — narrow lanes, the Gallery Walk, and shopfronts that have been trading since long before "artisan" was a word anyone needed. Eagle Heights Butchery has been one of them for around 26 years.

The shop sits in a small strip on Southport Avenue with easy-access parking right out front, next door to the FoodWorks. Locals drive up from as far as Loganholme for it, and visitors staying in the mountain's Airbnbs are pointed here for one reason: nothing here is mass-produced, and everything on the counter was made by someone who'll tell you exactly how to cook it.
